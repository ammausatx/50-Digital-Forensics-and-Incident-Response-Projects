# 50 Digital Forensics and Incident Response Projects

A practical portfolio of 50 authorized, hands-on DFIR exercises. Projects progress from evidence handling and endpoint triage to memory, network, cloud, and enterprise incident response.

> **Safety and evidence note:** Work only with systems, images, logs, and captures you own or are explicitly authorized to examine. Preserve originals, document custody, and hash evidence. All included illustrations are simulated training visuals—not screenshots or evidence from real investigations.
